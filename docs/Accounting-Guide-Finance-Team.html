Accounting on Yannis: Quick Guide for Finance & Team

Welcome. This guide shows where accounting lives and how to use it day to day. Most of what you need is under Accounting in the left sidebar.

Note: Finance (cash remittance, disbursements) is a separate menu. This guide is for the Accounting group only.

1. Where to find things

What you need Where in the left menu
List of all accounts (the chart) Accounting → Chart of Accounts
Opening balances at go-live Chart of Accounts → Post Opening Balances / View Opening Balances
Which account auto-posting uses Accounting → Account Mappings
Manual journal entries Accounting → Journal Entries
Vendor / staff expense claims Accounting → Expenses
Match bank statement to ledger Accounting → Bank Reconciliation
Full ledger lines by account Accounting → General Ledger
Check debits = credits Accounting → Trial Balance
Income vs expenses for a period Accounting → Profit & Loss
Assets vs liabilities & equity Accounting → Balance Sheet
Cash / bank movement Accounting → Cash Flow

Tip: if you do not see a menu item, ask an admin for accounting / ledger access.

2. Who does what

Role Main job in accounting
Finance / Accountant Set up accounts and mappings, post journals, approve expenses, reconcile the bank, run reports
Heads / Managers Submit expenses when needed; they may see Expenses but not the full ledger
Auditor View ledger and reports (read access)

Everything in Accounting is double-entry: every posting must balance (debits = credits).

3. One-time setup (do this before day-to-day use)

Do these in order.

Step 1: Check the Chart of Accounts

Go to Accounting → Chart of Accounts.

The system already seeds a standard Nigerian-style chart (Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Revenue, Cost of Sales, Expenses, Tax, and so on).

  • Review the list
  • Add any extra accounts your company needs
  • Edit names if needed
  • Leave group accounts as groups; post only to leaf (postable) accounts

Until the chart looks right, do not rely on reports.

Step 2: Set Account Mappings

Go to Accounting → Account Mappings.

This tells the system which GL account to use when it auto-posts (sales, stock, tax, expenses, and so on).

  • Review each mapping
  • Change a mapping only if your chart uses a different account than the default
  • You can reset a mapping back to default if you make a mistake

Wrong mappings send money to the wrong account. Fix this before go-live.

Step 3: Post Opening Balances (go-live only)

From Chart of Accounts, open Post Opening Balances.

  • Enter each account’s balance as at your go-live date
  • Debits and credits must balance (any leftover goes to Opening Balance Equity)
  • Post once when you are ready to start on the system

After this is posted, use normal journals and expenses for new activity. Do not treat opening balances as a monthly task.

4. Day-to-day flow

1. Post activity (journals / expenses / auto-postings) ↓ 2. Check General Ledger / Trial Balance ↓ 3. Reconcile the bank ↓ 4. Read Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow

A. Journal Entries (manual postings)

  1. Open Accounting → Journal Entries
  2. Create a new entry
  3. Add lines: each line is a debit or a credit to an account
  4. Total debits must equal total credits
  5. Save

Statuses you will see:

Status Meaning
Draft Saved, not yet in the ledger reports as posted
Posted Approved / live on the ledger
Cancelled Rejected or reversed; not active

If your role can approve: open a Draft entry → Approve and post.
If a posted entry was wrong: use reverse / cancel (do not silently edit history).

B. Expenses

  1. Open Accounting → Expenses
  2. Submit a vendor / expense claim (amount, details, proof if required)
  3. Finance reviews: Approve if correct, or Reject with a reason if not

Statuses: Pending → Approved or Rejected.

C. Bank Reconciliation

  1. Open Accounting → Bank Reconciliation
  2. Click New Reconciliation
  3. Choose the bank account and statement date / closing balance
  4. Match each bank statement line to the matching ledger entry
  5. When everything that should match is matched, Complete reconciliation

Do this regularly (for example monthly) so the bank account in the books matches the real bank statement.

D. Reports (read and check)

Use these after postings are in:

Page Use it for
General Ledger Drill into one account’s movements
Trial Balance Confirm the books balance (debits = credits)
Profit & Loss Profit for a period
Balance Sheet Position as at a date
Cash Flow How cash / bank moved over the period

Pick the date range (or as-at date) at the top of each report before you share numbers.

5. Simple monthly checklist

  • Chart and Account Mappings still look correct
  • All needed journal entries for the month are posted
  • Pending expenses reviewed (approve or reject)
  • Bank reconciliation completed for the month
  • Trial Balance balances
  • Review Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow

6. Quick reminders

  • Setup order: Chart of Accounts → Account Mappings → Opening Balances (once) → then daily work
  • Never post unbalanced journals (debits must equal credits)
  • Bank recon is matching the statement to the ledger, not creating sales
  • Opening balances are for go-live only
  • Menu labels only: use Accounting → … in the sidebar (no need to type URLs)
