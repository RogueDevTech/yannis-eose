Payroll on Yannis: Quick Guide for HR & Team

Welcome. This guide shows where things live and how monthly payroll works. Most of what you need is under HR in the left sidebar.

1. Where to find things

What you need Where in the left menu
Monthly payroll runs (main page) HR → Payroll
Create a new month’s payroll HR → Payroll → click Generate Monthly Batch
Pay roles, formulas, bonuses, tax HR → Payroll Config
Staff onboarding / account details HR → Staff Onboarding (HR review). Staff update their own details after login / onboarding.
Outside contractors (fixed fee) HR → Contractors
Paid payslip PDFs HR → Payslips
Reports / payroll register HR → Payroll Reports
Your own bank / payout details Config → My Profile (or during staff onboarding)

Tip: if you do not see a menu item, your account may not have access. Ask an admin to grant the right HR / payroll permission.

2. Who does what

Role Main job in payroll
Head of CS / Marketing / Logistics Create the draft for their department, check the numbers, submit to HR
HR Set up pay roles and formulas, confirm staff completed onboarding with account details, review the batch, approve and send to Finance (or reject with a reason)
Finance Pay the batch, mark it paid, export bank pay file
All staff Complete onboarding and keep bank / account details up to date

Payroll is monthly. One batch = one branch + one department + one month.

3. One-time setup (do this before the first run)

Do these in order.

Step 1: Create pay roles and formulas (HR first)

Go to HR → Payroll Config.

  • Create each pay role
  • Set the formula (salary, commission, tiers, and so on)
  • Set product bonus tiers and tax bands if you use them
  • Assign people to the right pay role from here

Until this is done, generating payroll will be empty or wrong. This is always the first setup step.

Step 2: Make sure everyone finished onboarding with their account details

Every staff member should update their onboarding with their account / bank details (and any other required fields).

  • Staff: complete onboarding after login, or update details under Config → My Profile
  • HR: check progress under HR → Staff Onboarding and confirm people are ready before the first payroll run

Do not run payroll until people have their account details filled in. Finance needs this to pay.

Step 3: Add contractors (only if you have them)

Go to HR → Contractors.

  • Add name, monthly fee, and bank details
  • These are fixed-fee people, not normal PAYE staff

4. Monthly payroll flow (the main path)

1. Generate draft ↓ 2. Check & adjust (Draft) ↓ 3. Submit to HR ↓ 4. HR approves → Finance ↓ 5. Finance pays → Mark paid ↓ 6. Payslips & reports ready

Step 1: Generate the month

  1. Open HR → Payroll
  2. Click Generate Monthly Batch
  3. Choose month, branch, and department
  4. Use Preview if available, then Generate

You land back on the Payroll list. Batches are grouped by month.

Step 2: Open the batch and check it

  1. On HR → Payroll, find the month
  2. Click View on the batch (or tap the card on phone)
  3. Review staff payouts, totals, and bank info on each line

While status is Draft:

  • You can Re-generate from latest data if numbers look wrong
  • Heads prepare and check their department
  • HR can add line adjustments when the batch is with HR (bonus, deduction, etc.)

Step 3: Submit to HR

On the batch page, click Submit to HR.
Status moves: Draft → Submitted to HR.

Step 4: HR review

HR opens the same batch from HR → Payroll.

  • If good: Approve & send to Finance
  • If not: Reject and write a clear reason (batch goes back one step; it is not deleted)

Status moves: Submitted to HR → Approved by HR (waiting for Finance).

Step 5: Finance pays

Finance reviews batches waiting for payment, exports bank pay if needed (Export bank pay on HR → Payroll), then Mark batch paid with a payment reference.

Status moves: Approved by HR → Paid.

After paid, every payout in that batch is paid, and payslips become available.

Step 6: Payslips and reports

  • HR → Payslips: download PDFs for paid lines
  • HR → Payroll Reports: register, cost by branch/role, trends

5. Status meanings (simple)

Status you see Meaning
Draft Created, still being checked. Not with HR yet.
Submitted to HR / Pending HR Waiting for HR to approve or reject.
Approved by HR / Pending Finance Waiting for Finance to pay.
Paid Money marked as paid. Payslips ready.

Reject always goes one step back, with a reason. The batch stays in the system.

6. Adjustments (extra pay or deductions)

On HR → Payroll, HR / Finance can use the Adjustments tab and Add-on for extras outside the normal formula (bonus, extra shift, etc.).

On a batch waiting for HR, you can also adjust a single person’s line from the batch detail page.

7. Short checklist for each month

  • Pay roles and formulas are correct in Payroll Config
  • Staff have updated onboarding with their account / bank details
  • Each Head (or HR) generates their department batch on Payroll
  • Draft checked → Submit to HR
  • HR approves → Finance
  • Finance pays → Mark paid
  • Download payslips / reports if needed
